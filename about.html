Our Story

About Midnight Ink

Built on the KZN South Coast, Midnight Ink exists for people who want a tattoo that's actually theirs — designed, discussed and delivered with the same care you'd expect from any craft worth doing properly.

The Artist

A decade of dedicated linework.

Midnight Ink is the studio of a self‑driven, formally trained tattoo artist with over ten years behind the needle. What started as an apprenticeship in a small coastal shop grew into a private studio built around one idea: tattooing is a craft, and it deserves the same patience, hygiene and honesty as any serious trade.

Today the studio works almost entirely on custom commissions — no flash walls, no rushed walk‑ins. Just a private space, a proper consultation, and a piece built to suit your skin, not a catalogue.
